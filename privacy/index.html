Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

CSD Good Services LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates this landing page to provide information about our commercial and residential painting services in South Florida and to allow interested parties to request a quote. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and submit your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our landing page, we collect two types of information:

  • Information You Provide: When you fill out our quote request form, you provide personal information such as your Full Name, Company Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Property Type, Service Needed, Location (City/ZIP), Approximate Size, Project Timeline, and any additional message you choose to include.
  • Tracking and Device Information: To optimize our digital advertising campaigns, we automatically collect certain technical data. This includes your IP address, browser type, and attribution data (such as Google Ads Click IDs [GCLID], UTM parameters, and the specific ad variation you interacted with).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for the following business purposes:

  • To evaluate your project requirements and provide an accurate painting or maintenance quote.
  • To contact you via phone, email, or SMS regarding your inquiry.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our Google Ads campaigns and understand which ads generate the most interest.

3. SMS Communications (TCPA Compliance)

By providing your phone number and submitting the form, you expressly consent to receive calls and text messages (SMS) from CSD Good Services LLC regarding your quote request and project details. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP".

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses standard web technologies, including Local Storage, to securely store ad attribution data (like GCLID and UTMs) on your browser for up to 90 days. This allows us to know which advertising campaign brought you to us, even if you navigate between the English and Spanish versions of the site before submitting the form. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking cookies for cross-site profiling. We use Google Tag Manager (GTM) to process conversion events when a quote is successfully submitted.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to outside parties. Your data is only shared with our trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • GoHighLevel: Our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system where your quote request is securely stored so our team can follow up with you.
  • Google LLC: We share conversion events (such as form submissions) with Google Ads to optimize our advertising delivery.
